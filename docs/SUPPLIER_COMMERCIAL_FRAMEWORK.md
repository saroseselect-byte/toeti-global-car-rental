# TOETI Supplier Commercial Framework — Founding Partner Pilot

Status: supplier-review framework. Commercial figures become binding only after written acceptance by TOETI and the supplier.

## Fixed operating principles
- €0 setup fee and €0 fixed monthly fee for the founding-partner pilot.
- Supplier remains the actual rental/experience provider and keeps control of inventory, pricing, availability, operating conditions and final booking confirmation.
- TOETI acts as an additional distribution channel; no exclusivity or minimum inventory/booking commitment is required unless separately agreed in writing.
- Pilot flow is request-to-book: traveler requests, supplier confirms/declines live availability, TOETI communicates the outcome.
- Supplier's own cancellation, no-show, refund, deposit, insurance/excess and operating conditions remain supplier inputs and are not replaced by invented TOETI rules.

## Payment architecture for supplier review
To minimize launch risk, the preferred founding-pilot architecture is supplier-direct settlement unless a supplier and TOETI explicitly agree a TOETI-collected payment flow in writing. Under supplier-direct settlement, the supplier receives the customer payment under the supplier's agreed booking/rental terms; TOETI does not hold supplier funds or create an artificial payout delay.

## Commission
The commission percentage and commission calculation base must be explicitly inserted in the supplier-specific commercial schedule and accepted in writing before activation. No universal percentage is treated as agreed until the owner approves one.

## Processing fees
Where the supplier collects payment directly, payment-processing fees are those of the supplier's chosen payment method/provider unless separately agreed. TOETI will not add an undisclosed transaction fee.

## Cancellations, no-shows, refunds and chargebacks
- Customer cancellation/no-show: supplier's accepted cancellation/no-show policy governs the customer-facing entitlement; payment/refund execution follows the party that collected the funds.
- Supplier cancellation: supplier confirms the cancellation and the customer receives the refund/alternative required by the accepted supplier terms and applicable law; the collecting party executes the refund.
- Chargebacks/payment disputes: handled by the party whose payment account processed the customer transaction, with cooperation from the other party for booking evidence.

## Customer communication
TOETI may handle marketplace/request status communication. Supplier remains responsible for service-specific operational instructions, rental/experience delivery, safety requirements and supplier-controlled terms. The exact support split can be recorded in the supplier-specific schedule.

## Activation gate
Nothing becomes active until the supplier has received and accepted in writing: supplier identity, offer/inventory data, prices, availability/confirmation method, customer-facing conditions, commission percentage/base, payment route, processing-fee allocation, cancellation/no-show/refund responsibilities and any supplier-specific support obligations.

## Supplier onboarding checklist
Rental cars: vehicles/rates, pickup/drop-off, availability method, deposit/payment rules, insurance/excess, driver requirements, cancellation/no-show/refund terms, API documentation if relevant.

Experiences: selected experiences, prices, meeting/pickup point, schedule/duration/capacity, inclusions/exclusions, availability method, cancellation/no-show/refund terms, safety/operator requirements.
